coverage/  -  functional coverage results
=====================================================================

Put here at sign-off:
  the exported coverage report    docs/j3_mm_rd/coverage_report.html -> Export
  the simulator coverage summary of your final regression (if your
    simulator writes one), named after the regression date
  diagrams/                       images (see diagrams/README.txt)

Sign-off: 100 % of the covergroups / coverpoints / crosses defined in your
test plan, or each hole justified in the coverage report. Code coverage is
not required.
