coverage/diagrams/  -  images for the coverage report
=====================================================================

  coverage_summary.png  (or .svg / .jpg)
      summary of your covergroup results; the name matches the image slot
      of docs/j3_mm_rd/coverage_report.html
  <covergroup>_<what>.png
      any further screenshot, e.g. a hole you justify in the report

Keep each image readable at 100 % zoom (names and percentages visible).
